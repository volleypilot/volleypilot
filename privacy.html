1. What We Collect

VolleyPilot does not collect or store any personal information. All your teams, scores, and settings remain private on your device.

Optional Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (app sessions, feature usage, device info).

  • This is optional and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Analytics data is completely anonymous, never linked to you, and used only to improve the app.

2. Data Storage

  • Local data: All your game data stays on your device.
  • Analytics data: Stored securely by Google Firebase for up to 14 months.

3. Your Controls

  • Disable analytics anytime in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Delete app data: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.

4. Privacy Compliance

  • GDPR compliant – EU users can request data deletion.
  • CCPA compliant – We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Contact volleypilot.app@gmail.com for any privacy requests.

5. Children's Privacy

VolleyPilot does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) – for optional anonymous usage tracking. Privacy policy
  • No other third-party services or trackers are used.

7. Security

Local data is protected by your device's security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). Analytics data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

8. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Updates will be reflected in the app and the "Last Updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email us: volleypilot.app@gmail.com
